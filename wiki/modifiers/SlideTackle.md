# Slide Tackle

<p align="center"><b>Type:</b> Universal Modifier</p>

> Slide tackle the nearest player, stunning them briefly.

The Slide Tackle can be assigned to any role. Its holder gets a **Tackle** button (bottom-left
modifier slot): using it lunges them at the nearest player and knocks them down. The victim can't
move, use abilities, or report for a short duration — but they can still be killed while down.
Careful who you tackle: an alerted Veteran will kill you for it, and a shielded Elusive will send
you across the map.

Like all universal modifiers, it's only assigned when its **Amount** option is above `0` (it ships
off by default) and requires TOU-Mira's non-vanilla role assignment.

## Ability

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Tackle** | Lunge at the nearest player and stun them briefly. |

## Options

- **Slide Tackle Amount** — default `0`
- **Slide Tackle Chance** — default `50%`
- **Tackle Cooldown** — default `30` seconds
- **Stun Duration** — default `2` seconds
