# Apparater

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/Apparater.png" width="128" alt="Apparater icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Crewmate &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Crewmate Power</p>

> Teleport to places around the map.

The Apparater can open a map overlay and click any location on the ship to teleport there instantly.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Teleport** | Open the map. Click anywhere on it to teleport to that location. |

## Options

- **Teleport Cooldown** — default `6` seconds
- **Max Uses** — default `4` uses (set to `∞` for unlimited)

## Origin

An original role created for Super Squad Among Us.
