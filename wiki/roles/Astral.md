# Astral

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/Placeholders/Impostor.png" width="128" alt="Astral icon (placeholder)"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Impostor &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Impostor Concealing</p>

> Pass through walls with ease.

The Astral can phase into an invisible spirit that walks through walls and can still kill. When the form ends, you are pulled back to the spot you phased from and remain invisible for a few seconds longer to slip away. Once you enter the phase, you cannot cancel it early—the tether runs its course.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Phase** | Become an invisible spirit that walks through walls and can still kill. When the form ends, you return to where you phased from and remain invisible briefly. |

## Options

- **Astral Form Cooldown** — default `25` seconds
- **Astral Form Duration** — default `15` seconds
- **Invisibility Linger After Return** — default `5` seconds
- **Astral Can Vent** — default `Off`
- **Astral Dies Without a Kill** — default `Off` (when on, phasing without killing during the form will cause death instead of returning normally)
