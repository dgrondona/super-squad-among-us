# Daddy Hagrid

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/Placeholders/Crewmate.png" width="128" alt="Daddy Hagrid icon (placeholder)"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Crewmate &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Crewmate Protective</p>

> Hide players in your cloak to keep them safe.

Daddy Hagrid can tuck the nearest player into his cloak. A hidden player vanishes from the game —
invisible, untargetable, unable to act — and rides along with Hagrid until the hide duration runs
out, popping back out at his position. Unlike a certain hungry bird, hidden players are never
harmed: a meeting releases them unharmed to vote, and if Hagrid dies or leaves, they're released
alive on the spot.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Hide** | Tuck the nearest player into your cloak. They're hidden and protected until they pop back out. |

## Options

- **Hide Cooldown** — default `25` seconds
- **Hide Duration** — default `15` seconds
- **Max Uses** — default `3` (`∞` available)
