# Elusive

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/Placeholders/Crewmate.png" width="128" alt="Elusive icon (placeholder)"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Crewmate &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Crewmate Protective</p>

> Send your attackers somewhere far, far away.

The Elusive can raise an invisible shield. While it's up, anyone who interacts with them — a kill
attempt or a targeted ability — is blocked and instantly teleported to a random spot on the map.
Attackers get no warning the shield is up until they've already been whisked away.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Shield** | Raise a shield for a short duration. Anyone who interacts with you while shielded is teleported to a random spot on the map. |

## Options

- **Shield Cooldown** — default `25` seconds
- **Shield Duration** — default `10` seconds
- **Max Shields** — default `5`
