# Eraser

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/Eraser.png" width="128" alt="Eraser icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Impostor &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Impostor Support</p>

> Mark players and strip them of their role - immediately or at the next meeting.

The Eraser is a cunning Impostor strategist with the power to erase other players' roles. Silently mark your targets during the round — they won't even know they're marked. By default, every marked player loses their modded role and becomes a plain vanilla Crewmate without any notification when the meeting's exile phase ends; the host can instead switch the Eraser to strip the role the instant it's used. Each use increases your cooldown permanently, so choose your targets wisely, and you only get a limited number of erases per game.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Erase** | Mark a player to lose their role — by default at the next meeting's end, or instantly if the host has enabled immediate erasing. |

## Options

- **Erase Cooldown** — default `30` seconds
- **Max Uses** — default `2` uses (set to `∞` for unlimited)
- **Eraser Can Erase Non-Crewmates** — default `Off`
- **Erase Immediately** — default `Off` (when on, the role is stripped instantly instead of waiting for the next meeting)
