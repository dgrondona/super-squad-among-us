# Godfather

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/Godfather.png" width="128" alt="Godfather icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Impostor &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Impostor Killing</p>

> Kill all Crewmates.

The Godfather is the head of the Mafia and uses the standard Impostor kill and sabotage buttons. The Godfather spawns as part of a three-person Mafia team along with the Mafioso and Mafia Janitor. A key mechanic of the Mafia hierarchy is that the Mafioso cannot act while the Godfather is alive—when the Godfather dies, the Mafioso's kill and sabotage abilities unlock immediately. The Mafia trio spawns together via a single spawn-chance roll and cannot spawn any other way.

## Abilities

The Godfather uses the standard Impostor kill and sabotage buttons—no dedicated ability button. The core mechanic is the Mafia hierarchy: the Mafioso is restricted until the Godfather falls.

## Options

This role has no configurable options. The Mafia trio spawns based on a server-wide **Spawn Chance** setting (default 50%).

## Origin

Ported from [TheOtherRoles](https://github.com/TheOtherRolesAU/TheOtherRoles) — the role design, button art, and effect sprites originate there, re-implemented on MiraAPI/TOU-Mira.
