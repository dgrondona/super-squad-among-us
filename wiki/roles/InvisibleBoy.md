# Invisible Boy

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/InvisibleBoy.png" width="128" alt="Invisible Boy icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Crewmate &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Crewmate Support</p>

> Turn invisible when no one is watching.

The Invisible Boy is always invisible when no living player can see him. The moment anyone can see even a sliver of him—whether they're standing in the room or coming around a corner—he becomes fully visible. Invisible Boy sees himself as a ghostly outline so he can still navigate. Dead players don't count as watchers, but living players in vents do. When invisible, you're hidden from other players, security cameras, and the admin table.

## Abilities

The Invisible Boy has no action button. This ability is always-on.

**Unseen.** While no living player can see you, you are invisible—hidden from players, cameras, and the admin table. You see yourself as a ghostly outline. Dead players don't count; players in vents do.

## Options

This role has no configurable options.

## Origin

An original role created for Super Squad Among Us.
