# Mafia Janitor

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/MafiaJanitor.png" width="128" alt="Mafia Janitor icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Impostor &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Impostor Support</p>

> Work with the Mafia by hiding dead bodies.

The Mafia Janitor is the cleanup expert of the Impostor team. Unlike other Impostors, you have no kill button — your specialty is removing bodies. Use your clean button to instantly erase dead bodies from the game, one at a time. This prevents crew from reporting bodies and helps the Mafia evade suspicion. The Mafia Janitor always spawns as part of a complete Mafia trio alongside the Godfather and Mafioso, never alone.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Clean** | Remove a dead body from the game. |

## Options

- **Clean Cooldown** — default `30` seconds
