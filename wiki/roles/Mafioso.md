# Mafioso

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/Mafioso.png" width="128" alt="Mafioso icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Impostor &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Impostor Killing</p>

> Work with the Mafia to kill the Crewmates.

The Mafioso is a member of the Mafia under the Godfather's command and uses the standard Impostor kill and sabotage buttons. However, while the Godfather is alive, both your kill and sabotage buttons are disabled and hidden from view. When the Godfather dies, both buttons unlock immediately—your kill cooldown does not reset, preserving your existing state. The Mafia trio spawns together via a single spawn-chance roll and cannot spawn any other way.

## Abilities

The Mafioso uses the standard Impostor kill and sabotage buttons—no dedicated ability button. The core mechanic is the Mafia hierarchy: while the Godfather is alive, you cannot use these buttons. When the Godfather dies, they unlock immediately.

## Options

This role has no configurable options. The Mafia trio spawns based on a server-wide **Spawn Chance** setting (default 50%).

## Origin

Ported from [TheOtherRoles](https://github.com/TheOtherRolesAU/TheOtherRoles) — the role design, button art, and effect sprites originate there, re-implemented on MiraAPI/TOU-Mira.
