# Ninja

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/Placeholders/Impostor.png" width="128" alt="Ninja icon (placeholder)"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Impostor &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Impostor Killing</p>

> Surprise and assassinate your foes.

The Ninja is an elite Impostor assassin who operates with stealth and precision. Mark a target to track their location, then strike from anywhere on the map to eliminate them instantly. After a successful assassination, become invisible for a brief moment to escape undetected. Your arrows leave visual traces at both your launch and landing points, creating a forensic trail that observant players might discover.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Mark** | Mark a target player and see their trace on the map. |
| **Assassinate** | Kill your marked target and become invisible. |

## Options

- **Mark Cooldown** — default `30` seconds
- **Knows Target Location** — default `On`
- **Trace Duration** — default `5` seconds
- **Trace Color Fade Duration** — default `2` seconds
- **Invisibility Duration** — default `3` seconds
- **Ninja Can Vent** — default `Off`
