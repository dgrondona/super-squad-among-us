# Pelican

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/Placeholders/Neutral.png" width="128" alt="Pelican icon (placeholder)"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Neutral &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Neutral Killing</p>

> You're soooo hungry.

The Pelican is a lone predator that hunts by consuming other players. When you devour someone, they're hidden from the game and dragged along with you. At the next meeting, all devoured players die — their bodies are gone, digested. If you are killed mid-round, everyone in your stomach is released alive and unharmed. You win when you're the last player left standing.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Devour** | Consume a player to remove them from the game. |

## Options

- **Devour Cooldown** — default `15` seconds
- **Pelican Can Vent** — default `Off`
- **Pelican Has Impostor Vision** — default `On`
