# Sentinel

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/Sentinel.png" width="128" alt="Sentinel icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Neutral &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Neutral Killing</p>

> Eliminate those who stand in your path.

The Sentinel is a Neutral Killer with a destructive arsenal. You can kill normally like any other killer, but your real power is explosives — detonate yourself to incinerate everyone nearby. The explosion radius is adjustable, letting you control collateral damage. Win by being the last one standing, or by ensuring all non-killers are gone.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Explode** | Explode all players nearby. |

## Options

- **Kill Cooldown** — default `25` seconds
- **Explode Cooldown** — default `60` seconds
- **Explosion Radius** — default `0.25x`
- **Sentinel Has Impostor Vision** — default `On`
- **Sentinel Can Vent** — default `Off`

## Origin

An original role created for Super Squad Among Us. It also serves as a reference implementation of this addon's role/button/option pattern, alongside Apparater.
