# Vulture

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/Vulture.png" width="128" alt="Vulture icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Neutral &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Neutral Evil</p>

> Eat corpses to win.

The Vulture hunts the dead, feasting on corpses left behind. Each body you consume is removed from the game and counted toward your meal threshold. Once you've eaten enough bodies, you win immediately — the game ends the moment you reach your target. While alive, blue arrows point you toward every dead body on the map, guiding you to your next meal.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Eat** | Consume a dead body, removing it from the game and increasing your meal count toward victory. |

## Options

- **Eat Cooldown** — default `15` seconds
- **Bodies Needed to Win** — default `4`
- **Vulture Can Vent** — default `On`
- **Vulture Sees Body Arrows** — default `On`
