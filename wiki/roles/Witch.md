# Witch

<p align="center"><img src="../../SuperSquadAmongUs/Resources/RoleIcons/Witch.png" width="128" alt="Witch icon"></p>

<p align="center"><b>Team:</b> Impostor &nbsp;|&nbsp; <b>Alignment:</b> Impostor Support</p>

> Cast a hex upon your foes.

The Witch is a powerful Impostor curse-caster. Channel a hex on your nearest target, and if the channel completes, the target becomes cursed and will die at the next meeting's exile phase. No notification reveals who hexed them — only a hex icon next to their name during meetings gives it away. If you are voted out before the meeting resolves, your hexes are saved and targets still die. Each successful hex increases your cooldown, rewarding careful, strategic play over reckless casting.

## Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Hex** | Curse a player to die at the next meeting. |

## Options

- **Hex Cooldown** — default `30` seconds
- **Additional Cooldown** — default `10` seconds
- **Can Hex Anyone** — default `Off`
- **Cast Duration** — default `1` second
- **Trigger Both Cooldowns** — default `On`
- **Voting Out Witch Saves Targets** — default `On`
- **Witch Can Vent** — default `On`
